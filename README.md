# Silent Forging

[![Discord](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1448257/description_c5e501e5-328a-46c7-a594-bb0c451254a5.png)](https://discord.gg/xTeHR2tdYh)

A [Silent Gear](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/silent-gear) addon that replaces instant part crafting with a hands-on **forging minigame**, inspired by TerraFirmaCraft.

## Features

- **Tool Forge block** — place it, drop in a blueprint and a matching ingot or gem, and forge the part yourself.
- **Six-stroke minigame** — shape a heat bar with hammer strokes (Hit, Bend, Draw, Extrude, Fold, Temper) and land inside the recipe's target range.
- **Quality system** — efficiency matters. Reach the target in the fewest strokes for **Pristine** parts (+10% stats); waste strokes and end up **Crude** (-10% stats).
- **Full Silent Gear integration** — finished parts feed straight into normal gear assembly.
- **JEI support** — recipes and targets viewable in-game.

## How It Works

1. Place the **Tool Forge** and right-click to open it.
2. Insert a Silent Gear **blueprint** and a **matching ingot or gem**.
3. Press keys **1-6** to apply strokes and steer the bar to the recipe's target.

Full walkthrough lives in the in-game **GuideME** book.
